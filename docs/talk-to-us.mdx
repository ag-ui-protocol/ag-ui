AG-UI is free and open source. You can use the protocol with your own client,
agent framework, and infrastructure without CopilotKit or a paid service. For
bugs, feature requests, contributions, and free community help, visit
[Support for AG-UI](/support).

CopilotKit provides open-source frontend SDKs and a runtime for building AG-UI
applications. Teams can also choose its optional commercial product,
**CopilotKit Intelligence**, or engage CopilotKit for engineering assistance.

## CopilotKit Intelligence

Intelligence adds integrated product infrastructure to a CopilotKit application.
Its **AG-UI Streams** store and replay interaction history, helping users reopen
conversations and reconnect to active runs. Your agent framework can continue
managing its own conversation history and model context.

The integrated implementation handles event storage, replay, and the connection
back to a live run, reducing the delivery infrastructure your team has to build
and maintain. Cloud-hosted Intelligence also lets CopilotKit operate the platform;
self-hosting is available when your team needs to operate it in its own infrastructure.
Intelligence also offers capabilities such as Automatic Learning, which turns
real usage into skills your team can review and publish.

AG-UI event streams remain part of the open protocol. You can implement
persistence and reconnection yourself using AG-UI's
[serialization model](/concepts/serialization) and your own infrastructure;
these capabilities do not require Intelligence.

The documented Intelligence setup uses a working CopilotKit frontend, Runtime,
and agent connected to an Intelligence project. It is not a drop-in service for
every AG-UI client. Cross-device access requires your application to provide a
stable, authenticated user identity. Runs can continue after a browser disconnects
while the Runtime and agent remain running; this is not recovery after a server failure.

- [Explore CopilotKit Intelligence](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/intelligence/overview)
  for product capabilities and deployment options.
- [AG-UI Streams](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/threads) explains delivery behavior
  and links to existing guides for adding Streams to framework threads.
- [Open source vs Intelligence](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/concepts/oss-vs-enterprise)
  compares the open-source stack with the optional product layer.
- [Intelligence quickstart](https://docs.copilotkit.ai/intelligence/quickstart)
  covers integration, credentials, and authentication prerequisites.

## Commercial engineering support

If your team wants hands-on architecture or implementation help with a
production deployment, CopilotKit offers optional commercial engineering
support. This is a separate service from the Intelligence product and from
using or contributing to the AG-UI open-source project.
